# Chat Wrapped application surface

## Mode

Persuade first, then Operate. The landing must make the result desirable and the primary action obvious; the setup and story screens then become a focused task flow.

## Direction contract

## FORM

- Concept roll seed: `ea049fc6` (direction scope, Persuade mode, assigned grounded candidate 3).
- Carrier: one house-party confession deck, expressed as stacked 9:16 scorecards and repeated through the complete product flow.
- Composition: simultaneous offer-and-proof on desktop; offer first, then proof card on mobile; centered story frame with utility controls receding around it.
- State vocabulary: deal, choose, cut, read, reveal, unlock, customise, save, and share.
- Responsive rule: preserve the story card’s 9:16 geometry and move supporting controls below it rather than compressing the card.
- Motion rule: directional card travel and blur serve story navigation only; all other states change directly.

### World

A house-party confession deck: every reveal feels dealt, flipped, scored, or stamped. Saturated card stock, layered sheets, chunky display type, and tightly controlled playful motion carry the full product—not a generic analytics dashboard wearing a bright gradient.

### First viewport

The offer and proof coexist immediately. The left side states “Your group chat, exposed” and starts the flow; the right side is a working-scale 9:16 3AM reveal card with real synthetic data, stacked over two offset cards. On mobile, the offer lands first and the proof card follows in the same scroll without a secondary marketing section.

### Visitor path

Deal the premise → choose whose chat → follow platform-specific export directions → drop or sample a file → watch the analysis deck resolve → swipe three free reveals → preview the locked reveal → purchase → customise → export or share.

### Signature interaction

The story deck is the one authored interaction: horizontal card changes use directional blur and travel, progress bars track position, and pointer swipes, taps, keyboard arrows, and optional six-second auto-play all control the same state.

### Cross-surface reach

The deck grammar extends to selection cards, phone-export steps, loading, payment, theme swatches, story cards, and share/export states. Quiet utility surfaces use the same compact radii, dark ink, saturated action colour, and direct voice without imitating a physical card unnecessarily.

### Honest risk

The party-deck concept could make a privacy-sensitive tool feel unserious. Trust language therefore stays visible on landing, export, upload, processing, paywall, and sharing; controls remain literal and accessibility behavior stays conventional.

## Grounded source

The assigned third grounded direction was the scorecard and confession-card ritual of Nigerian house parties and group hangouts. It scored well for audience identification and product clarity while meeting the PRD’s pinned bold type, saturated gradients, chunky rounded cards, emoji, and 9:16 story frame.

## Challenger decisions

- Torn flyer wall: competitive on audience energy, weaker on product clarity. Its total material commitment raised the deck system, but its tearing motif was not borrowed.
- Seven-segment displays: competitive for the 3AM reveal, weak as a durable whole-product world. Its discipline around fixed positions informed story progress and numeric alignment.
- Memory quilt: declined on audience identification and conversion clarity. Its provenance discipline raised the persistent chat/date footer.
- Mesophotic dive: declined on audience identification. Its staged descent raised the sense of progressive reveal.
- PC-98 field: declined because its rigid interface language conflicts with fluid mobile accessibility and the pinned typography.
- Data-sublime field: declined because it turns a social, funny product cold. Its respect for precise data presentation raised tabular numerals and real supporting stats.
