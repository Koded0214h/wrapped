# Chat Wrapped quality bar

The build should feel as committed as a professionally art-directed mobile story campaign while remaining as immediately usable as WhatsApp’s own export flow.

## Required evidence

- A first-time visitor understands the upload-to-story offer, privacy promise, and primary action within the first viewport.
- The 9:16 proof card is content, not decoration: it contains believable synthetic names and stats in the same visual system as the product output.
- The confession-deck world governs layout, type, state, and motion across landing, setup, story, paywall, customisation, and sharing.
- Story cards remain screenshot-worthy at 390px, preserve readable hierarchy, and export cleanly at 1080 × 1920.
- Locked cards reveal a redacted headline while keeping real names and values out of the DOM.
- Every theme has readable contrast against both gradient endpoints and its own surface token.
- Keyboard focus, Escape, focus trapping/restoration, arrow-key story navigation, and reduced motion all work without a pointer.
- No fabricated claims, testimonials, or real chat content appear; sample data is visibly synthetic.

## Failure threshold

A generic SaaS hero, an analytics-dashboard card grid, broken 9:16 geometry, an illegible locked reveal, decorative motion, or a privacy promise that disappears during conversion is a material failure.
